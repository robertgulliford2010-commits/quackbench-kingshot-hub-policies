# QuackBench Kingshot Hub — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 22 October 2026  
**Service/operator:** QuackBench Kingshot Hub / QuackBench  
**Privacy contact:** **quackbenchofficial@outlook.com**

> **Scope:** This policy describes data involved in the QuackBench website, Discord bot and any enabled Appointments, Reminders, Redeemer or Translator modules. Some capabilities described below are planned; we only collect information needed for features actually enabled. Before paid public launch, we must confirm the responsible legal operator's full name and postal contact details, actual hosting regions, the production data-retention schedule and any formal processor arrangements.

## 1. Who is responsible for information

QuackBench operates the shared service, account system, platform security and subscription administration. Contact us at **quackbenchofficial@outlook.com** for information about who is legally responsible for a particular processing activity or to make a privacy request.

Depending on how an organization uses a product, a participating kingdom or Discord server's administrators may independently determine what player or community information to collect, approve, publish or retain. They may act as separate controllers for those decisions while QuackBench supplies the technical service under applicable arrangements. This distinction will be formalized in the relevant paid-service/data-processing terms before launch. Discord and payment processors also process personal information under their own policies.

## 2. Personal data we may collect

**Accounts and sign-in:** Discord user ID, display name, avatar, server membership/authorization information relevant to installation, login times, session identifiers and role/permission snapshots. For owners, co-owners taking over billing and payers, a verified email address may be required.

**Players and Appointments:** in-game Kingshot Player ID and player name, selected kingdom/alliance, submitted availability, preferred appointment days, resources and speedup information, requests, staff decisions, allocations, trade offers, status changes, linked/guest session identifiers, invitation-link uses and relevant supporting screenshots or temporary verification evidence. Associated profile-to-kingdom history may be retained to enforce authorized transfers and guard against impersonation.

**Discord server setup:** server, channel, role and administrator identifiers; which services are connected or enabled; settings and moderation/configuration events needed to operate and secure each service.

**Reminders:** event titles, scheduled times, recurrence settings, reminder text, embeds, uploaded images/icons, role mention choices and send-attempt outcomes.

**Redeemer:** voluntarily linked Kingshot account identifiers, codes considered or attempted, statuses and diagnostics required to avoid duplicate attempts and investigate failures. Eligibility information or errors may reveal game account status. We do not need your personal Discord password or Kingshot password.

**Translator:** configured channel IDs, message text and associated message/user metadata needed to translate permitted messages, translation results, language settings, moderation controls and operational diagnostics. The system should process only configured and authorized sources; messages may be briefly stored or cached where technically necessary. We do not intend to build unrelated advertising profiles from message content.

**Billing and support:** payer contact details, Stripe/Discord customer or transaction/entitlement IDs, plan and payment status, invoices or receipt references, service assignments, renewal dates, support messages, ownership-transfer case references, contact attempts, evidence and objections. Full payment-card numbers and CVVs are handled by the payment provider and should never be sent to QuackBench support.

**Technical/security information:** IP addresses where made available to us, device/browser information, service request logs, error traces, timestamps, rate-limit activity, and suspicious-access indicators. We may use necessary security and authentication cookies or equivalent sessions. We will separately explain and request permission for non-essential analytics/marketing cookies if introduced.

## 3. Where information comes from

We receive information directly from you when you sign in, submit forms, configure services, connect a game profile, upload files or contact support. We may also receive data from Discord APIs and authorized server events, payment providers, other players or a kingdom's administrators, and technical hosting/security systems. If a player submits another person's details, the submitter and approving kingdom staff must have a lawful basis or appropriate permission to do so.

## 4. Why we use personal data and UK GDPR lawful bases

The appropriate lawful basis varies by use:

| Purpose | Typical lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Provide login, requested services, player actions and authorized subscription features | Performance of a contract where the person is party to it; otherwise legitimate interests in providing the requested community service |
| Keep records of purchases, invoices and legally required disclosures | Legal obligation and, where relevant, contract |
| Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized transfers, duplicate code attempts or security incidents | Legitimate interests in protecting users, service integrity and lawful access |
| Operate admin approvals, configured Discord announcements and community records | Legitimate interests in operating requested functions, subject to rights and relevant organizational-controller decisions |
| Respond to customer service, complaints, disputes and ownership-transfer requests | Contract, legitimate interests and legal obligation, as relevant |
| Optional marketing and non-essential tracking, if introduced | Consent or another lawful basis where legally permitted, with required opt-outs |

Where legitimate interests apply, our interests include secure operation, preventing misuse, preventing unauthorized disclosure, resolving service disputes and delivering tools requested by participating communities. We will assess proportionality and privacy impacts when introducing new processing.

We do **not** claim that joining a Discord server automatically means a user has consented to all optional processing. Server administrators must appropriately inform their members about enabled translation and announcement features.

## 5. Automated processing and AI

Where enabled, AI may analyze provided screenshots or pasted forms, infer requested structured information, and produce translations. This processing can be wrong and should not replace human review of important appointment or ownership decisions. We do not intend to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about individuals. Ownership-transfer overrides require human authorization and recorded reasons.

For AI-enabled features, relevant user-provided content may be sent to authorized AI processing providers. The exact provider, retention settings, processing location and applicable safeguards must be confirmed before the feature goes live and reflected in this policy or a supplementary notice.

## 6. Who may see information

- **Within a kingdom:** authorized staff may see the information needed for their roles; some appointment status or timetable details may be visible to invited players where expressly enabled. Guest access is restricted and does not unlock other kingdoms' data.
- **Within a Discord server:** authorized server administrators may manage Reminders, Redeemer and Translator; posted messages, mentions and translations may be visible in their configured channels according to Discord settings.
- **Payers:** can see their own subscription and billing records, but payment status does not grant access to other organizations' operational data.
- **QuackBench support and Superadmin:** may access relevant records only as necessary for authorized support, fraud/security investigations, service integrity and documented ownership-transfer cases, with access logging.
- **Service providers:** may receive limited necessary data to perform hosting, databases, file storage, bot communications, payment processing, mail delivery, error logging or AI functions.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. Discord and independent checkout providers operate under their own privacy notices for their platform services.

## 7. Providers and international transfers

Our planned technical stack includes Discord, Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare, Stripe and, for optional AI features, an AI service provider such as OpenAI. Exact vendors may change before launch, and not every provider is used by every customer or feature. Processing may take place outside the UK. Where required, we will assess adequacy decisions, the UK's international data transfer agreement/addendum or other recognized safeguards before transferring personal data abroad.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the relevant purpose, using the following criteria:

- Account/permission/active service data: for the active account, approved association or product subscription, plus a limited recovery/closure period.
- Appointment and game records: for active kingdom operations and requested historical availability, then subject to the organization's authorized retention or deletion choice and applicable legal/security needs.
- Temporary verification images and translatable message content: only for the processing or documented abuse investigation that requires them, not indefinitely by default.
- Support cases and ownership-transfer evidence: while the dispute is active and for a proportionate post-resolution period necessary to protect the parties' rights, demonstrate the outcome and handle legal claims.
- Billing/tax records: for the period legally required for accounting, tax, fraud or contractual records.
- Operational logs, backups and cached data: according to documented rolling retention schedules and technical recovery limits.

**Before commercial launch** we will publish actual durations or sufficiently specific criteria for each production category. A deletion request may not erase information that must be retained by law or to defend legitimate claims, but such retained information will be access-restricted and not reused for unrelated purposes. Backup deletion may follow a rolling cycle.

## 9. Security

We plan role-based access, kingdom and server isolation, database row-level security, transport encryption, private file access, abuse controls, backups, permission rechecks, and audit histories for privileged actions. These protections reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Never share full card details, passwords, one-time codes or privileged bot tokens with support or other players.

## 10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis. Contact **quackbenchofficial@outlook.com**, ideally noting your Discord user ID, affected server/kingdom and request. We may need to verify identity or authority before releasing protected records, and we will not expose another customer's records to satisfy a request.

You may complain to the UK **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ . You may also contact your local supervisory authority where relevant.

Requests involving kingdom-controlled records may need coordination with the organization's responsible administrator or controller. Changing Discord settings or cancelling a subscription does not necessarily delete already posted Discord messages or legally retained invoices.

## 11. Children and age restrictions

The service is intended only for people eligible to use Discord and the relevant Kingshot/game features under applicable rules. We do not intentionally solicit information from people who are not eligible to use the service. Contact the privacy email if you believe ineligible individuals' data has been included so we can review and act appropriately.
